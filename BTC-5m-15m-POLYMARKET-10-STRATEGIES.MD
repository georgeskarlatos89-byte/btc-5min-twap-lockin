### S1 — TWAP LOCK-IN CONVERGENCE ("the round is already decided, the book hasn't noticed")
**Markets:** 5m + 15m · **Style:** late-round taker · **Edge class:** settlement math

**Causal edge:** Resolution is the round's cumulative TWAP vs the open print. With 45–90s left, most of that TWAP is already locked — but the last minute of spot only moves the resolving value by its small remaining time-weight. The crowd anchors on the *last spot tick* and keeps paying for reversal chances that the math doesn't support. **Losers: lottery-ticket buyers on the trailing side and last-tick chasers.** Fee-friendly because entries happen at extreme prices where the fee is 0.14–0.63%.

**Mechanics**
- Inputs: sub-second spot composite (Kraken/Coinbase WS) + the round's open print; reconstruct accumulated TWAP (`TWAP_acc`) continuously.
- Trigger: t ≤ 45s remaining (5m) or ≤ 90s (15m). Compute `gap = TWAP_acc − open` in bps; required remaining move to flip ≈ `gap / (t_rem / T)`; convert to P(flip) using trailing 4h realized σ (today: 7 bps/5m).
- Entry: take the ask on the leading side when `P_model(lead) − ask − fee(ask) − 0.01 > 0.02` (2¢ post-fee margin). Example seen today: book at 0.85 with ~3.5 min left while lock-in math implied >0.95.
- Exit: hold to resolution (round is short; exit friction would eat the edge).
- Size: $10–25/trade, max 1 position per round.

**Backtest:** replay `prices-history` (1s fidelity) + Kraken 1m for 500+ rounds; rebuild `TWAP_acc`; simulate fills **only at the ask actually shown, only inside the live band, fee per formula** (the Part-4 gate rule). Vary the margin threshold 1–4¢.
**Incubate:** 2 weeks, $10, max 2 trades/hour.
**Kill:** ≥200 trades and post-fee realized edge < 0.5¢, or win-rate misses model by >2σ. **Decay note (P1):** this is the most obvious post-TWAP idea — assume it crowding and require the speed of your TWAP reconstruction to beat the book's reprice.

### S2 — OPEN-PRINT DISPLACEMENT ("first 15 seconds: the open reference is known, the book is still 50/50")
**Markets:** 5m + 15m · **Style:** round-open taker/maker hybrid · **Edge class:** reference-data latency

**Causal edge:** Each round resolves vs the Chainlink price *at the round's start*. Seconds after the bell, spot is already displaced from that open print, but the book is still quoting near 50¢ (verified: next-round book sits at 0.50/0.51 before the bell because the book doesn't clear). Whoever captures the open reference + early tape first trades against stale quotes. **Losers: stale resting orders and bettors entering at 50¢ without knowing the displacement.**

**Mechanics**
- At t=0: snapshot the open reference (Chainlink stream; fallback: exchange composite at the bell).
- At t=5–15s: `disp = spot − open`. σ of a 5m round ≈ 7.5 bps today, so |disp| ≥ 5 bps at t=10s is already ~0.7σ of directional info.
- Entry: if disp ≥ +5 bps and Up ask ≤ 0.60 → take Up (or, better, quote maker at model fair −1¢ to earn rebate); symmetric for Down. Skip if |disp| < 5 bps (noise zone — P3, waiting is the edge).
- 15m variant: higher threshold (~8 bps) because more time remains.

**Backtest:** thousands of past rounds from Gamma (resolutions) + Kraken 1m interpolation for displacement at t≈10s. Question to answer: *does a 5–8 bps displacement at t=10s predict outcome often enough to clear the 1.68% fee at 60¢?*
**Incubate:** $10, only on qualifying rounds (expect selectivity — most opens will be sub-threshold on a Saturday tape like today).
**Kill:** if threshold must be pushed >10 bps to be profitable (that's a latency race you're losing, not an edge).

### S3 — FEE-FARM TWO-SIDED MAKER WITH CASCADE PULL ("get paid by the 1.75% the gamblers pay")
**Markets:** 5m + 15m · **Style:** mid-round market making · **Edge class:** liquidity provision / fee recycling

**Causal edge:** Takers pay 1.75% at 50¢; makers pay 0%, earn spread, a pro-rata share of the 20% rebate pool, and qualify for liquidity rewards (≥50 shares within 4.5¢). ~30% of rounds (measured today) never leave ±3 bps — pure farming rounds. **Losers: entertainment bettors crossing the spread + fee; they keep paying because the product is the gamble.**

**Mechanics**
- Quote Up @ 0.49 and Down @ 0.49, ≥50 shares each, from t+30s until 60% of round elapsed.
- **Pull both quotes instantly when:** (a) `all_liq_10m` prints a one-sided cascade ≥ $1.5M in any 10s window (the 1s-lag feed — the only liquidation feed allowed intra-round); (b) spot moves ≥6 bps in 10s; (c) orderflow imbalance flips extreme (background check, 90s cadence).
- Inventory rule: if one side fills and the other doesn't within 20s, mark-to-model and offer the inventory side at breakeven; never "hope" a position (P5: encode, don't intend).
- Session gate: run **only** in quiet-regime hours (right now: all day Saturday; generally Asia session + dead US lunch).

**Backtest:** simulate quote fills against `prices-history` touches; count adverse-selection events (fills followed by ≥10 bps runs); net = spread captured + est. rebates − adverse losses. Baseline variance of fill counts must be measured before setting any alert threshold (M5 lesson).
**Incubate:** $10/side, 2 weeks, log every pull-trigger to verify the pull logic fires on real cascades, not noise.
**Kill:** 2 consecutive weeks where adverse selection > gross capture.

### S4 — LIQUIDATION CASCADE CONTINUATION (15m only)
**Markets:** 15m · **Style:** mid-round taker · **Edge class:** forced-flow momentum

**Causal edge:** Liquidations are *forced* market orders; cascades self-propagate for 1–3 minutes as margin calls chain. The multi-exchange aggregate feed detects this with 1s median lag — inside a 900s round that's an eternity. The Polymarket book reprices slower than the cascade unfolds. **Losers: faders of forced flow and stale quotes.**

**Mechanics**
- Trigger: `all_liq_10m` shows ≥ $1.5M one-directional liquidations within 10s **and** spot confirms direction (both conditions — a liq print without spot follow-through is a absorbed wall, skip it).
- Entry: buy continuation side if `ask ≤ P_model − fee − 0.02`; P_model from measured post-cascade drift in your own event study (don't assume).
- Exit: hold to resolution if TWAP gap locks in (handoff to S1 logic); else sell into the model reprice.
- **5m explicitly excluded:** detect+fill+resolve inside 300s leaves no runway; the knowledge base's own measurement (Binance feed 163s stale = half a 5m round) is the warning about how little time these rounds give.

**Backtest:** harvested liq snapshots exist since 2026-09-07 — run the event study first: average continuation 60s/180s after cascade onset, hit-rate by cascade size. Then map onto Polymarket quotes' adjustment speed.
**Incubate:** $10/trade; triggers will be rare on today's baseline-funding tape — that's correct behavior, not a bug (P3).
**Kill:** <55% post-fee win rate over 100 triggers, or if funding/OI regime shows cascades have become mean-reverting (they can, at extremes).

### S5 — WICK FADE / TAPE-LAG REVERSION
**Markets:** 5m (early two-thirds only) · **Style:** fast taker or maker exit · **Edge class:** microstructure lag

**Causal edge:** When spot wicks (spike + fast reversion), Polymarket makers reprice to the spike but re-quote the reversion slower — for 1–5s the just-reverted side is mispriced in the wick direction. **Losers: momentum chasers buying wick tops; slow re-quoters.**

**Mechanics**
- Detect: spot +8 bps in ≤5s, then ≥60% retracement within 10s (or symmetric down-wick).
- Entry: buy the *opposite* of the wick side if its ask is still ≥ model fair + 2¢.
- Hard time gate: only while t < 60% of round elapsed. Late in the round TWAP lock-in makes fading dangerous (S1's domain) — this is explicitly an early/mid-round strategy. The two never compete for the same seconds.
- Why-now grounding: this morning's tape is exactly wick-friendly — BTC chopped −0.41% over 4h with 43% up-share, weekend book thin (top depth ~300–600 shares on 5m), so overshoots are larger and slower to correct.

**Backtest:** Kraken 1s-equivalent reconstruction (1m bars are too coarse → harvest 1s spot going forward for 1–2 weeks before trusting this backtest; say so honestly rather than backtesting on interpolated data).
**Incubate:** $10, max 3/day.
**Kill:** if measured mean-reversion completion time > your average fill-to-fill latency + quote adjustment (i.e., you're structurally last).

### S6 — COIN-FLIP HARVESTER AT ROUND OPEN
**Markets:** 5m + 15m · **Style:** maker at the bell · **Edge class:** structural flow + tie rule

**Causal edge:** Every round re-opens a fresh 50/50 gamble and a steady entertainment cohort plays it (the book at 0.50/0.51 before the bell is them). Empirically P(Up) = 50.0% (n=144 windows measured today) and **ties resolve Up** (`≥`) — an epsilon every modeler sees, almost no bettor prices. The taker fee is at its *maximum* (1.75%) exactly where these bettors trade; makers pay nothing and receive rebate-pool share. **Losers: coin-flip takers paying full fee for a fair bet.**

**Mechanics**
- Quote both sides at 0.49, capped $30/side, first 45s of the round.
- If one side fills: try to complete the pair — if the other side can be filled such that combined cost < $0.97, take it (locked ~3% + rebates). Otherwise carry the single leg to resolution: it's a fair coin you sold at 0.49 with rebate tailwind and the tie epsilon.
- Never quote past t+45s (after that, S1/S2 information starts arriving and you'd be the stale one).

**Backtest:** fill asymmetry is the key question — simulate whether your 0.49 quotes get hit *symmetrically* or mainly by informed flow right before directional runs. Use `prices-history` touches + resolutions.
**Incubate:** $10/side, 2 weeks, track conditional fill asymmetry (did my Up-fill rounds resolve Up more than 50%? If yes, someone is picking me off).
**Kill:** conditional asymmetry > 8pp over 300 fills.

### S7 — 5m/15m OVERLAP CONSISTENCY ARB
**Markets:** both, traded as a pair · **Style:** market-neutral model arb · **Edge class:** segmented cohorts

**Causal edge:** A 15m round shares 10 of its 15 minutes with the three 5m rounds inside it, yet the two books are populated by different cohorts (measured today: 5m median vol $49.8k vs 15m $29.8k — **the 15m book is the thinner, more mispriceable one**). When a joint TWAP model says the two implied probabilities are inconsistent by more than fees + spreads, one book is wrong. **Losers: whichever cohort is trading the thinner book off vibes.**

**Mechanics**
- Maintain one model: current accumulated TWAP state + remaining-time distribution (σ from trailing regime) → P(Up_5m) and P(Up_15m) jointly.
- Flag when `|P_model(15m) − ask_15m| − fee − 0.01 > 0.03` **and** the 5m book is consistent with the model (use the 5m book as the truth-check; trade the 15m side).
- Prefer maker entries (0.47–0.55 zone) to keep fees at zero; taker only above 4¢ mispricing.
- Correlation caution: both legs are the same underlying — net exposure per 5m window capped (see Part 3).

**Backtest:** calibration first — does the joint model's 15m probability have better Brier score than the 15m book's own price? If not, there is no arb; stop. If yes, simulate capture.
**Incubate:** $10, only flagged rounds.
**Kill:** model Brier ≤ book Brier, or realized capture < fees+spread over 100 flags.

### S8 — SESSION REGIME ROUTER ("Asia fades, US momentum, macro blackout")
**Markets:** both · **Style:** gate/meta-strategy layered on all the others · **Edge class:** regime conditioning (P4: regime change is the edge)

**Causal edge:** A single bot trades all hours identically while the participant mix rotates: Asia = retail-heavy quiet tape (today's regime: σ 7 bps, 30% flat rounds — fade-friendly), US 13:30–21:00 UTC = vol doubles and trend rounds appear (momentum-friendly), scheduled releases (8:30/10:00 ET data, 14:00 FOMC) = vol events where directional taker trades are the *sucker* trade. **Losers: time-blind bots and humans applying one playbook to all sessions.**

**Mechanics (all in code — P5):**
- Asia block (00:00–12:00 UTC): enable S3, S5, S6; disable S4.
- US block: enable S1, S2, S4, S5; S3 quotes widen or pull.
- Macro calendar hardcoded: ±1 round around each scheduled release = **no directional taker entries**; only S10 box mode allowed.
- Realized-vol override: trailing-1h 5m σ > 1.5× its 7-day baseline flips the router to US-mode regardless of clock.
- Today-specific: Saturday → no US data releases at all; the router should run Asia-mode all day. That's a live test case.

**Backtest:** per-session attribution on the underlying strategies' signals — does the gate lift any of them? If gating adds nothing in backtest, don't ship it.
**Incubate:** rides on the other strategies' $10 incubations; cost = zero extra.
**Kill:** per-session PnL attribution shows no improvement vs ungated after 3 weeks.

### S9 — WALLET COATTAILS (profitable-trader follow, price-gated)
**Markets:** 5m + 15m · **Style:** fast taker · **Edge class:** revealed informed flow

**Causal edge:** The `profitable-traders` feed hands over the top Polymarket wallets by 7d PnL — the leader prints $6.6M PnL on 496 trades in 7 days, a signature consistent with exactly these short-horizon crypto books. Their fills on `btc-updown-*` tokens are observable; following while the price is still inside the fee-adjusted band drafts their research late and cheaper. **Losers: whoever was on the other side of the whale's fill.**

**Mechanics**
- Track the harvested wallet list on the CLOB activity of the two current round tokens.
- Follow rule (strict): observed fill → within 2s → current ask ≤ whale fill price + 1¢ **and** model EV still positive after fee. Otherwise skip — a follow at a worse price is donating.
- Per-wallet sizing by rolling 30-day hit-rate on these books; wallets rotate off the list automatically (P1: the copied edge decays as more people copy; the list must churn).
- Hard rule from knowledge base: this is a *candidate* edge, not a proven one — it gets the most skeptical backtest of the ten.

**Backtest:** reconstruct historical fills of listed wallets (Polymarket data API / on-chain), measure: how often is the price still available +2s later, and what's the conditional win-rate. If availability < 40%, latency kills it before incubation.
**Incubate:** $10/follow, cap 10 follows/day.
**Kill:** ≤ 0 post-fee edge after 100 follows, or wallet hit-rates degrade within your own sample (you're watching alpha decay live — document it).

### S10 — VOL-EVENT BINARY BOX ("own the whipsaw, don't guess it")
**Markets:** 15m primarily (releases land inside one 15m candle) · **Style:** both-sides maker / pair taker · **Edge class:** convexity in event rounds

**Causal edge:** In rounds containing scheduled releases (or live cascade regimes), probability mass migrates to the extremes while the book still shows 50±1¢. Two harvests: **(a) pair arbitrage** — buy Up + Down whenever combined ask < $0.97 post-fee (panic on one side, nobody requoting the other — occurs reliably in whipsaws); **(b) maker straddle** — quote Up @ 0.45 and Down @ 0.45 around the event; the whipsaw fills both legs, $0.90 of cost pays $1.00. **Losers: panic takers on both sides of the same whipsaw — they pay twice, once each direction.**

**Mechanics**
- Calendar gate (S8 feeds it): rounds containing 8:30/10:00 ET data or 14:00 FOMC → box mode armed from round-open.
- Vol gate: trailing-1h realized σ > 1.8× baseline arms box mode even off-calendar (cascade regimes).
- Leg-risk rule: if only one leg fills and the other hasn't filled within 60s, offer the missing leg aggressively up to combined $0.97; beyond that, unwind the lone leg at model fair. Never carry a naked event position (that's S4/S5's job, not this one's).
- **Why-now grounding:** nothing fires today (Saturday, no releases) — this is the Monday-and-later playbook; today is used to calibrate the vol-gate threshold against a release-free baseline.

**Backtest:** historical release rounds (identify by timestamp) → measure two-leg fill rate and average combined cost. Also measure pair-sum < $0.97 frequency in cascade rounds from harvested data.
**Incubate:** $10/leg, first live test at the next US-session data release.
**Kill:** two-leg fill rate < 30% of armed rounds (you're running a naked directional book in disguise), or lone-leg unwind losses > pair gains over 20 events.

## PART 3 — PORTFOLIO ASSEMBLY & RBI EXECUTION PLAN

**Uncorrelated groups (P2 — three bots on correlated edges is one bot with extra steps):**

| group | strategies | what earns | correlation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — liquidity/fee farming** | S3, S6, S10b | taker fees, rebates, rewards; short vol | quiet-tape dependent |
| **B — settlement math** | S1, S7 | TWAP lock-in vs crowd anchoring | independent of direction |
| **C — fast flow** | S2, S4, S5, S9 | latency + forced-flow information | vol-dependent |
| **Router** | S8 | gates everything by regime | meta |

They fail in different weathers: A dies in vol spikes, C dies in dead tape, B is roughly regime-neutral. That's the point.

**Global rules — all encoded in code, none in willpower (P5):**
1. **Live-band gates execute before any fill is recorded** (the Part-4 bug that faked a +5.71pp headline: paper fills bypassed MIN/MAX_LIVE_ASK). Every backtest and every paper run must fail loudly if a fill is logged above/below the band.
2. Fees computed per official formula on *every* simulated and live fill; no entry without post-fee margin ≥ threshold.
3. Same-underlying exposure cap: max **$30 net across both markets in any 5-minute window** (they share the underlying — a cascade blows both up simultaneously).
4. Feed-health flat-switch: if Moon Dev monitor M3/M4/M7 fires (stale/empty/dead-key), S4 and S9 go flat immediately.
5. Daily stop per strategy; a strategy at stop for 3 consecutive days is paused, not widened.
6. No trading on interpolated or unmeasured data (S5 waits for 1s spot harvest; S9 waits for fill-availability measurement).

**RBI sequence:**
- **R:** done above — each strategy names who pays and why.
- **B:** one afternoon, 25 versions: parameterize thresholds (margins, σ multipliers, cascade sizes), run all ten backtests against `prices-history` + Kraken + harvested feeds. Promote the top 3–4 by post-fee expectancy **subject to group diversification** (don't incubate three group-A bots and nothing else).
- **I:** $10 real money per trade, 2–4 weeks, then compare live band to backtest with honest CIs — remember the knowledge base's own number: n must actually be large enough before any "SIGNIFICANT" is written down. Scale only what matches.

**Expected attrition:** per P1 and the 2026-08-07 history of these exact markets, at least some of these ten will decay or get killed by a mechanics change. The asset being built is the pipeline (harvester → backtester → gated incubator), not any single parameter set.
